## Full Description

```
🌱 MADI — All-in-One Speech Therapy Manager

Korean speech-language pathologists (SLPs) juggle session notes,
assessments, IEPs, parent reports, and scheduling — all day, every day.

MADI brings all of it into a single app, with AI doing the heavy lifting.
Therapists spend less time on paperwork and more time with the child.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🎯 Who is it for?

• Speech therapy clinic directors (multi-staff, billing, role-based access)
• Licensed speech-language pathologists (1:1 and group therapy)
• Parents who want to follow their child's progress from home

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

✨ Key Features

📝 Quick Session Notes
• Voice dictation → auto-formatted with clinical vocabulary
• Photos, next-session memos, scoring on one screen
• Average 1–2 minutes per session log

🤖 AI Clinical Reports
• Convert standardized test results into clinical-grade reports
• Supports REVT, U-TAP, KOLRA and other Korean SLP assessments
• AI-suggested short/long-term IEP goals

📅 Integrated Scheduling
• Color-coded multi-therapist calendar
• Auto parent notifications (24h before / on changes)
• Attendance, discharge, and pause status in one tap

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Portal
• Home activity cards and weekly auto-generated reports
• Portfolio view of growth milestones
• Push notifications for stronger home-clinic continuity

🌙 Dark Mode & PWA Install
• Install to home screen for native-app feel
• Offline support for key flows
• Voice quick-log works without Wi-Fi

🔒 Security & Privacy
• TLS 1.2+ encryption for all traffic
• Row-Level Security separating clinic data
• Child (under 14) data requires guardian consent
• AI processing data is contractually purged on completion

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

※ Not a medical device. Does not diagnose or prescribe.
※ AI-generated reports require licensed therapist review before clinical use.
```
